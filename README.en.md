Drop the experience you think is unimpressive — "I just did a group project", "I helped a professor tidy up some data" — to Xiaoyi like a chat, and it helps you make sense of it, say it right, and write it into a résumé.

> Tell Xiaoyi your story in Chinese or English — it works in both.

## What Xiaoyi does for you

<img src="assets/mascots/owl-think.png" alt="Xiaoyi with a magnifying glass: find the right roles to target" width="46" align="left">

**Find the right roles to target** — not sure what to apply for? It helps you pick a direction, *actually searches the web* for live openings, decodes the requirements, and points out what you're missing.

<br clear="left">

<img src="assets/mascots/owl-magic.png" alt="Xiaoyi with a magic wand: translate experience into role language" width="46" align="left">

**Translate your experience into "role language"** — even the most ordinary experience, it digs out the highlights and says them in words recruiters understand. This is Xiaoyi's signature trick.

<br clear="left">

<img src="assets/mascots/owl-resume.png" alt="Xiaoyi holding a résumé: write résumé lines you can actually submit" width="46" align="left">

**Write résumé lines you can actually submit** — polish the highlights into punchy bullets, then assemble them into a finished résumé tailored to the role you're after.

<br clear="left">

## Six little helpers — use whichever you're stuck on

| Skill | What it does | Trigger keywords |
|---|---|---|
| [career-role-finder](skills/career-role-finder/) | Pick a direction + search the web for real openings | what to apply for, find internship, hiring now |
| [career-jd-analyzer](skills/career-jd-analyzer/) | Decode a job posting, including its "subtext" | JD, requirements, should I apply, what it wants |
| [career-gap-planner](skills/career-gap-planner/) | What you're missing, how to close it, how long it takes | what's missing, what to learn, study plan |
| [career-experience-mapper](skills/career-experience-mapper/) | Turn raw experience into language recruiters understand | résumé, experience, internship, career switch |
| [career-bullet-builder](skills/career-bullet-builder/) | Polish it into a punchy résumé bullet | résumé bullet, rewrite, quantify |
| [career-resume-tailor](skills/career-resume-tailor/) | Assemble a submission-ready résumé per role | tailor résumé, too long, one-page résumé |

## It even goes online to find jobs for you

Other tools just "teach you how to write". The **role-finder** here **actually goes online** — to [official campus-recruiting sites](https://jobs.bytedance.com/campus), [Nowcoder roundups](https://www.nowcoder.com/jobs/school/schedule), and [the GitHub Campus2026 aggregator](https://github.com/namewyf/Campus2026) — to **find real, currently-open roles that fit you, with real links**. If it finds none, it says so — it never invents a fake one; expired / closed postings get filtered out.

> **You:** I'm a junior CS student, I know Python, looking for a summer data internship in Beijing.
>
> **Xiaoyi:** I'd target "data analysis internships" (within reach); I searched and found a few that are open —
> · Company · Data Analysis Intern — real link (as of [month]; confirm it's still open before applying)
> · …(closed / expired ones won't be handed to you)

## The promise: it never makes things up

If it's not there, it says so — it won't invent employers, experiences, or numbers for you. **Pretty lies that fall apart the moment an interviewer probes only hurt you.** That's Xiaoyi's bottom line.

## Get started in 3 steps

1. Install [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code)
2. Run one line in your terminal (other helpers: see [install.md](install.md)):
   ```bash
   npx skills add agentsope/career-skills/skills/career-experience-mapper
   ```
3. Just tell it your situation, e.g.:
   > I have no internship; in class I did a group project on a social enterprise. I want to apply for a project-assistant role — can this go on my résumé?
