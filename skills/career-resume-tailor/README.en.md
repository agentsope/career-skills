# career-resume-tailor · assemble your experience into a submission-ready résumé

[简体中文](README.md) · English

> **One résumé for every role means it's aimed at none of them.**

Takes a pile of experiences, picks and orders them for the role you're targeting, and assembles a résumé you can submit.

## What it does for you

- Decides which experiences go first and which to cut, based on the role you're applying to
- Trims a new-grad résumé to one page, laid out cleanly
- Same experiences, different roles — produces a separate version for each

## A quick example

You have a pile of experiences and want an operations management-trainee role — it moves the most relevant ones up, trims the irrelevant ones, into a one-page résumé; say "I also want to apply for a data role" and it gives another version with a different emphasis — same experiences, different framing.

## How to use

Install it:

```bash
npx skills add agentsope/career-skills/skills/career-resume-tailor
```

Then just tell Claude, e.g.:

> Assemble these experiences into a résumé for an operations management-trainee role, and give me another version for a data role.

## Its bottom line

It only selects and emphasizes — it never invents experiences for you; you provide the real target role / JD, and it won't fake a link.
