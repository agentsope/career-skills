# career-experience-mapper · turn your experience into words recruiters understand

[简体中文](README.md) · English

> **It's not that you have no experience — you just can't "translate" it.**

Think "I just did a group project" or "I helped a professor tidy up some data" is too weak to mention? There's real, recruiter-relevant ability buried in there — you just haven't said it right. Tell it your experience and it digs out the highlights and puts them in résumé-ready words.

## What it does for you

- Spot what an experience actually demonstrates, and whether it's worth writing
- Turn plain, spoken descriptions into résumé wording recruiters understand
- No internship / switching fields? It still mines coursework, clubs, and part-time jobs for things worth writing

## A quick example

- You say: I took part in a group project about a social enterprise.
- It gives: Analyzed the financial sustainability and operational risks of a social-enterprise case; proposed cost-control recommendations (4-person course team).

It just states clearly what you did — without adding a single number.

## How to use

Install it:

```bash
npx skills add agentsope/career-skills/skills/career-experience-mapper
```

Then just tell Claude, e.g.:

> I have no internship; in class I did a group project on a social enterprise. I want to apply for a project-assistant role — can this go on my résumé?

## Its bottom line

If it's not there, it won't make it up; want an English version, just ask.
