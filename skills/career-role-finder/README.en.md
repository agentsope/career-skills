# career-role-finder · find your direction and the roles that are hiring

[简体中文](README.md) · English

> **The first step in a job hunt isn't fixing your résumé — it's figuring out what to apply for.**

Not sure what to apply for? It picks a direction based on your situation, and actually goes online to find roles that are open right now.

## What it does for you

- Suggests within-reach directions based on your background (no empty promises, not just the hot ones)
- Actually searches the web for open roles and gives you real links
- Lost, switching fields, no clue? It still helps you work out a direction from scratch

## A quick example

You say "I'm a junior CS student, I know Python, looking for a summer data internship in Beijing" → it suggests targeting data-analysis internships, and searches for a few currently-open ones to send you (filtering out any already closed).

## How to use

Install it:

```bash
npx skills add agentsope/career-skills/skills/career-role-finder
```

Then just tell Claude, e.g.:

> I'm a junior CS student, I know Python, looking for a summer data internship in Beijing — what can I apply for? Help me find some that are hiring.

## Its bottom line

If it can't find real openings, it says so — it never invents companies or links to bluff you; postings get taken down, so double-check before you apply.
