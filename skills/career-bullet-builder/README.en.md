# career-bullet-builder · polish your experience into a punchy résumé line

[简体中文](README.md) · English

> **It's not that you have nothing to write — you just haven't written the line that makes an HR pause.**

Takes a rough, or weakly-written, résumé sentence and polishes it into a line that's punchy and true.

## What it does for you

- Swaps flat openers like "responsible for…" / "took part in…" for strong, concrete wording
- Reads professional even without fancy numbers (no padding, no fabrication)
- Gives you two versions of a line — pick the one you like

## A quick example

- You wrote: Responsible for the club's official account; gained some followers.
- It gives: Ran the club's official account; planned a weekly content calendar and 3 themed campaigns, driving steady follower growth.

It didn't invent "how many" — give it the real number and it'll add it.

## How to use

Install it:

```bash
npx skills add agentsope/career-skills/skills/career-bullet-builder
```

Then just tell Claude, e.g.:

> Make this résumé line stronger: responsible for the club's account, gained some followers.

## Its bottom line

It never invents numbers for you; pretty lines that fall apart under interview questions don't get written.
